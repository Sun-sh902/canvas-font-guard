Scope & limits

In Canvas 2D and OffscreenCanvas, matching family names are swapped for a nonexistent internal name, so the browser falls back to ordinary fonts in the stack. Where available, matching fonts are also filtered out of local font enumeration.

System fonts are not uninstalled. DOM/CSS font metrics, unlisted families, WebGL and every canvas readback path remain outside scope, and pages may detect the extension itself.

Site access is requested only when you switch protection on, and is kept when you switch it off. Revoke it anytime in Chrome’s extension settings.
